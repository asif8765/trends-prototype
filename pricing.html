FAQ

Do I pay the fee on RTO?

No. The platform fee is charged on delivered orders only. Courier cost on a refused parcel is deducted from the reseller wallet.

Can I use Daraz on Basic?

Daraz and API access are gated to Agency in this example card. Shopify or WooCommerce is available from Basic (one store).

Is there a satisfaction window?

A 60-day subscription satisfaction guarantee is under consideration for launch. It is not a live offer in this prototype.
