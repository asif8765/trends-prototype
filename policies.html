Policies and help

These pages will be finalized before launch. The summaries below match the business blueprint and are not legal advice.

Terms of service

Resellers subscribe to import listings. Suppliers must ship genuine, accurately described goods on the stated SLA. is the COD collection and courier booking principal for platform orders, not the seller of record to the end customer.

Payouts, RTO, and refunds

Fees on delivered orders only. RTO courier cost is debited from the reseller wallet. Payouts run weekly to bank, Raast, JazzCash, or Easypaisa, subject to Account Health holds. Withholding tax, if applicable, will be shown on statements (to verify with counsel).

Account Health policy

Ladder: Healthy → Warning → At risk → Restricted → Suspended → Terminated. Thresholds are proposed in the product and labeled as such. Restrictions (order caps, listing limits, payout reserves) apply automatically. Termination and Plan of Action appeals are reviewed by a human. On termination, a proposed 90-day funds reserve applies. Content and video-use violations (using a supplier video without permission, or ripping another supplier's media) count on this same ladder.

Content and video-use policy

Suppliers may only upload videos they own, or have licensed, and must declare those rights. They set permissions per video: allowed in the reseller store, allowed in paid ads, and whether a watermark is required. Resellers may import and use a video only under those permissions. Using a video without permission, stripping a required watermark, or ripping another supplier's content is an IP / content-policy violation. A report and takedown flow hides the asset while it is reviewed; repeats lead to restriction. Trends does not pull videos from wholesalers who have not joined the platform.

Prohibited items

These categories are not allowed on . Do not apply to sell them, and do not list them. Listing a prohibited item is an Account Health violation and can lead to restriction or termination.

  • Medicines and prescription drugs ادویات اور نسخے کی دوائیں
  • Weapons ہتھیار
  • Counterfeit or replica brands نقل یا جعلی برانڈز
  • Adult items بالغ مواد
  • Hazardous or flammable goods خطرناک یا آتش گیر اشیاء
  • Live animals زندہ جانور
  • Tobacco and vapes تمباکو اور ویپس

Packaged, non-perishable grocery and health foods are allowed. Loose or fresh food, and anything that needs a pharmacy licence, is not.

Privacy

We store CNIC / NTN documents for KYC, phone numbers for OTP and confirmation, and order data for fulfillment. This prototype does not collect real personal data — do not upload genuine CNIC images here.

Help

Live product will have an AI chatbot with human escalation. In this demo, use the in-app toasts as stand-ins, or open the admin exception queue.
