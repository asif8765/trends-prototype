Money flow

How moves an order in Pakistan.

The platform sits in the middle: the reseller owns the customer relationship, the supplier owns the stock, and books the courier, collects COD, and splits the ledger.

For resellers

  1. 1. Subscribe and connect a store. Basic, Pro, or Agency. Connect Shopify or WooCommerce (Daraz on Agency). OTP login on phone + WhatsApp.
  2. 2. Import listings. Browse the catalog, see wholesale, MAP, courier estimate, door price, and profit. Download the ad pack. Edit your sell price (at or above MAP) and push to the store.
  3. 3. A customer pays COD. The order lands here. We confirm the buyer on WhatsApp or IVR before anyone packs.
  4. 4. You earn on delivery. After the courier remits COD, your margin (sell − wholesale − courier − platform fee) becomes available. Refused parcels (RTO) debit courier cost from your wallet — this is disclosed, not a surprise.

For suppliers

  1. 1. Apply and pass KYC. CNIC, NTN, bank or Raast, and warehouse photos. Auto-approval unless flagged.
  2. 2. List at wholesale + MAP. Set a daily order cap. Stock syncs to every reseller who imported the SKU.
  3. 3. Pack on SLA. 24h or 48h handover. We print the courier label. Late dispatch hits Account Health and can place a payout reserve.
  4. 4. Get paid after delivery. Wholesale is released when the parcel is delivered and COD is reconciled. You do not collect cash from the buyer.

Door-price formula

Door price = your sell price + estimated courier
Customer COD = door price
Reseller profit (if delivered) = sell price − wholesale − platform fee
Note: courier is paid from COD collected; RTO courier cost is deducted from the reseller wallet.
Example fee in this prototype: Rs 25 per delivered order (assumption / example until launch).
