Public policy stub: Policies — Account Health · Content and video use.

Content & video use: resellers may only use a supplier video in their store or in paid ads when that permission was granted. Using a video without permission, stripping a required watermark, or ripping another supplier's content is an IP / content-policy violation and can lead to restriction. Report and takedown is available from the listing. Trends does not pull videos from wholesalers who have not joined.
