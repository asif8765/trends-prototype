How it works

Four steps. One ledger.

01

Import

Pull listings and Urdu/English ad creatives into Shopify, WooCommerce, or Daraz. Edit price before you publish.

02

Confirm

We WhatsApp or IVR-confirm the buyer before the supplier packs. Fake-order patterns are scored automatically.

03

Deliver

The platform books a local courier by city and performance, tracks the parcel, and collects COD.

04

Split

Weekly payouts to bank, Raast, JazzCash, or Easypaisa. Every rupee sits on a per-order ledger.

Why

Built to avoid the free-app race to the bottom.

MAP + verified suppliers

Suppliers set wholesale and a minimum advertised price. Resellers who undercut get a warning, then a listing block. KYC (CNIC, NTN, bank, warehouse photos) is auto-reviewed; only flagged cases wait in a human queue.

Account Health on both sides

Amazon-style score cards, eBay-style status badges, and Walmart-style targets and windows — adapted to Pakistan COD: delivery ratio, confirmation rate, dispatch SLA, defects, and wallet balance.

Central COD escrow

We collect the cash, deduct courier and fees, and pay supplier share and reseller margin only after delivery. RTO cost sits on the reseller wallet, disclosed up front.

Creatives included

Each listing ships with an ad pack: images plus Urdu and English captions. Pro and Agency plans refresh packs automatically.
